Hi, I’m Shen (Sam) Xie, a student in Computational Science and Engineering at Georgia Tech, in the H. Milton Stewart School of Industrial and Systems Engineering.

I work on AI for scientific discovery, multimodal learning, and human–AI interaction, mostly applied to recommender systems and digital platforms. The short version: I try to make machines a bit better at doing science, and a bit better at understanding people.

Before Atlanta, I was a research assistant at HEC Paris with Prof. Xitong Li (remotely — the Paris part was mostly a time zone), at HKUST with Prof. Tat Koon Koh, and at Wuhan University with Prof. Baojun Gao. I did my bachelor’s in Politics, Philosophy, and Economics at Wuhan University with a minor in Computer Science and Engineering, so I can argue about institutions and loss functions with equal enthusiasm :)

Research interests:

  • AI for scientific discovery
  • Multimodal learning
  • Recommender systems
  • Human–AI interaction
  • Digital platforms & UGC

  1. AI for science

    General-Purpose LLM Agents for Efficient Scientific Experiment Selection

    Co-first author with Yuming Zeng

    Teaching LLM agents to pick the next experiment wisely: fewer experiments, more science.

    In progress · since Sep 2026
    details

    A general framework for sequential scientific experiment selection: an agent adaptively chooses the most informative next experiment from a large candidate space, aiming to reach a scientific objective with as few experiments as possible.

    Code in Github. Public soon
  2. Multimodal learning

    Diffusion-Based Image–Audio Alignment

    Third author · with Biyang Xin, Mingwei Sun, Shijie Zhang, and Xitong Li

    Diffusion models that learn what a picture sounds like, and what a sound looks like.

    In progress · since Aug 2026
    details

    A diffusion-based multimodal framework for learning aligned image–audio representations, enabling cross-modal correspondence and generation across visual and acoustic modalities.

    Code in Github. Public soon
  3. Multimodal learning

    EEG-to-Speech Decoding via Coordinate-Aware Cross-Modal Representation Learning

    First author · with Mingwei Sun, Shijie Zhang, and Xitong Li

    Decoding speech content from EEG, evaluated across subjects and content so the model cannot cheat.

    In progress · since Apr 2026
    details

    A unified EEG-to-speech-content framework that maps variable-channel EEG signals to shared MFCC and HuBERT speech representations using coordinate-aware CNN–Conformer encoders.

    Leakage-controlled multi-dataset pipelines with subject and content OOD splits, masked channel–time modeling, contrastive EEG–audio alignment, and counterfactual EEG controls.

    Code in Github. Public soon

  5. Digital platforms

    The Power of Content Similarity in Recent Reviews on Hotel Revenue

    Second author · with Baojun Gao and Jiawen Wang

    When recent reviews all sound the same, hotels earn less. Evidence from 1.5 million TripAdvisor reviews across 2,990 hotels in Texas.

    Manuscript in preparation
    details

    Higher similarity between adjacent recent reviews significantly reduces hotel revenue, with the strongest effects for high-end, high-volume, and lower-rated hotels.
