    General-Purpose LLM Agents for Efficient Scientific Experiment Selection

    Co-first author with Yuming Zeng

    How can an agent choose the most informative next experiment and reach a scientific objective with fewer experiments?

    In progress · Since Sep 2026

  2. Multimodal learning

    EEG-to-Speech Decoding via Coordinate-Aware Cross-Modal Representation Learning

    First author · With Mingwei Sun, Shijie Zhang, and Xitong Li

    Learning shared representations between variable-channel EEG and speech, with an emphasis on evaluation across subjects and speech content.

    In progress · Since Apr 2026
    More about this work

    Coordinate-aware CNN–Conformer encoders map EEG to MFCC and HuBERT speech representations. Evaluation uses subject/content out-of-distribution splits, masked channel–time modeling, contrastive EEG–audio alignment, and counterfactual EEG controls.
